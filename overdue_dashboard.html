How Updates Work

Notes are matched by invoice number

When you upload a new QBO file, the system:

  1. Matches each row by invoice number (the Num column).
  2. Preserves notes, collection status, and sent-to-collections date for invoices that already exist.
  3. Updates amount, open balance, dates, and client name from the new file.
  4. Adds any invoices that are new in the upload.
  5. Removes invoices that no longer appear (they've been paid/cleared). Their notes & collection state are archived in case they reappear.
